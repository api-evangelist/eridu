All seven entries below are projections of one surface, `https://eridu.ai/wp-json`, and all are
anonymous and read-only. The OpenAPI documents are API Evangelist derivations of the provider's own
live self-describing route index; Eridu publishes no OpenAPI.

- [Eridu Content API](https://eridu.ai/wp-json/wp/v2/posts): News and press archive. 1 published post at capture.
- [Eridu Pages API](https://eridu.ai/wp-json/wp/v2/pages): 13 static marketing and policy pages — Company, Leadership, Careers, Contact, Resources, Newsletter, Terms, Privacy.
- [Eridu Media API](https://eridu.ai/wp-json/wp/v2/media): Media library. Header reports 115 attachments; only 21 are anonymously retrievable.
- [Eridu Taxonomy API](https://eridu.ai/wp-json/wp/v2/categories): Post categories and tags. 1 of each; no custom taxonomies.
- [Eridu Search API](https://eridu.ai/wp-json/wp/v2/search): Cross-content search over posts and pages. 14 searchable objects.
- [Eridu Discovery API](https://eridu.ai/wp-json/): Self-describing route index — 167 routes across 7 namespaces — plus types, taxonomies, statuses and public authors.
- [Eridu oEmbed API](https://eridu.ai/wp-json/oembed/1.0/embed): oEmbed 1.0 provider endpoint for eridu.ai URLs.

## How to call it

- Base URL: `https://eridu.ai/wp-json`. No key, no signup, no OAuth.
- Read `GET /wp-json/` first — the route set is a function of the site's installed plugins and changes
  without notice.
- Pagination: `page` (1-based) + `per_page` (default 10, max 100); totals in `X-WP-Total` and
  `X-WP-TotalPages`. **On `/wp/v2/media` the total header disagrees with what you can read** — page
  until a short body, not until the header total.
- Shape the payload with `_fields=` and resolve relationships in one call with `_embed`.
- `context=edit` returns 401 anonymously. Use the default `context=view`.
- Errors are the WordPress envelope `{code, message, data:{status}}`, not RFC 9457. Match on `code`.
- No rate-limit headers are returned. Self-throttle.
- The `wp-abilities/v1` namespace is registered but returns 401 anonymously; its abilities cannot be
  enumerated without a WordPress application password.
